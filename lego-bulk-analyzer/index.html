Brick Lot Appraiser

Add photos of a second-hand bulk lot and the asking price. Claude picks out minifigures, valuable parts and likely sets, estimates what it all resells for, and tells you whether the lot is worth buying.

  • Spread pieces out on a plain surface rather than shooting the bag.

  • Each photo is shrunk to about 1.2 megapixels before Claude sees it, so several close photos beat one wide one.
